# Aurora Cloud Platform - Pricing

Aurora Cloud bills per month, per workspace. All prices are shown in Polish zloty (PLN)
and exclude VAT.

## Plans

| Plan       | Monthly price | Included compute hours | Vector storage | Support        |
|------------|---------------|------------------------|----------------|----------------|
| Starter    | 199 PLN       | 100 hours              | 5 GB           | Email, 48h     |
| Team       | 899 PLN       | 1,000 hours            | 100 GB         | Email, 8h      |
| Business   | 2,499 PLN     | 5,000 hours            | 1 TB           | 24/7 chat      |
| Enterprise | Custom        | Custom                 | Unlimited      | Dedicated TAM  |

## Overage

- Extra compute is charged at 0.35 PLN per hour.
- Extra vector storage is charged at 0.20 PLN per GB per month.

## Discounts

Annual billing receives a 15% discount on every plan. Registered non-profits receive an
additional 20% discount. Discounts do not stack with promotional credits.
