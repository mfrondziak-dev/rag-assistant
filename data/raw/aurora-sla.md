# Aurora Cloud Platform - Service Level Agreement

## Uptime commitment

Aurora Cloud commits to a monthly uptime of 99.95% for the multi-zone Compute service
and 99.9% for the Vectors service.

## Service credits

If Aurora fails to meet the uptime commitment, customers receive service credits:

- Below 99.95% but at or above 99.0%: 10% of the monthly fee.
- Below 99.0% but at or above 95.0%: 25% of the monthly fee.
- Below 95.0%: 50% of the monthly fee.

Credits must be requested within 30 days of the incident.

## Support response times

| Plan       | P1 (critical) | P2 (high) | P3 (normal) |
|------------|---------------|-----------|-------------|
| Starter    | 48 hours      | 72 hours  | 5 days      |
| Team       | 8 hours       | 24 hours  | 3 days      |
| Business   | 1 hour        | 8 hours   | 2 days      |
| Enterprise | 15 minutes    | 2 hours   | 1 day       |

## Planned maintenance

Planned maintenance is announced at least 7 days in advance and is excluded from
uptime calculations.
